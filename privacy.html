Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

OpenVID is designed to let visitors browse its video collections without creating an account. This policy explains what may be processed when you visit the site and when you contact us.

1. Information you provide

You can browse the site without registering. If you email us to suggest a video, report a problem, or make a removal request, we receive the information you include in that message, such as your email address, message, and any links or files you send. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

2. Technical information and hosting

When you visit a website, its hosting provider or infrastructure may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, requested page, and request time. This information may be used for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and service delivery, depending on the provider's configuration and policies.

OpenVID is currently hosted on Vercel and is planned to move to Codeberg Pages. The hosting provider may therefore change. We have not verified the exact logs retained or retention periods for every hosting configuration, so we do not promise that no technical logs are created. Please review the relevant provider's current privacy documentation for details about its processing.

3. Cookies and tracking

OpenVID is not intended to require an account or use personal profiles. This page does not claim that no cookies or technical storage can ever be used: that can depend on the site, browser, embedded resources, and hosting or third-party services in use. If analytics, advertising, or other tracking features are added, this policy should be updated to describe them before or when they are introduced.

4. Third-party links and media

The site may link to or load content from third-party websites or media hosts. When you visit those services or your browser requests their resources, those providers may receive technical information under their own privacy policies. OpenVID does not control their data practices.

5. How information may be used

Information sent to our contact email may be used to respond to you, review video submissions, investigate rights or safety reports, and maintain the site. Technical information may be processed by the hosting provider for the purposes described above.

6. Retention and security

Email correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain records. Technical-log retention depends on the hosting provider and configuration. No website or email service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent directly to OpenVID, contact contact.sandboxbrowser.dev@proton.me. We will review requests where we can identify and control the relevant information. Information held by a hosting provider or another third party may need to be addressed with that provider.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site, its hosting, or its features change. The current version will be published here.
