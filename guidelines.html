Video Submission Guidelines

To suggest a video for OpenVID, email contact.sandboxbrowser.dev@proton.me. We may decline or remove submissions that do not meet these rules.

Every submitted video must meet all of the following requirements:

  1. You have the legal authority to submit the video.
  2. The video is genuinely in the public domain or is otherwise lawfully available for the intended use. Do not assume that an old video is automatically public domain.
  3. The video does not contain illegal content.
  4. The video does not contain racist or discriminatory content, including racist jokes.
  5. The video is safe for work (SFW).
  6. The video does not contain copyrighted material unless you have the necessary rights for the intended use.
  7. The video does not contain advertisements or promotional placements.

If a video is removed from OpenVID, we cannot guarantee deletion of copies visitors have already downloaded or copies held by independent third parties.
